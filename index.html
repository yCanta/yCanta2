Welcome to yCanta

yCanta keeps your church's songs in one place. Find a song by its words, put songbooks together, print them as a PDF, and show the words on a screen. Once you've signed in, it works without internet too.

Start with Songbooks or All Songs above. Your name at the top right opens a menu with Settings and more. explains the rest.
